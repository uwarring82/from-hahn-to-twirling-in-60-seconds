# What does a spin echo average?
## A branch-resolved Ramsey test of Hamiltonian symmetrization and channel twirling with a single magnesium ion

**Perspective and experimental proposal · discussion draft · v0.3.0-dev.1 · 26 November 2026**

### Abstract

Coherent refocusing and channel twirling can use the same control group while yielding different measured spin coherences. We propose a Ramsey comparison on the clock transition of a single ²⁵Mg⁺ ion. A deterministic echo visits both elements of the group within one interrogation. A randomized protocol chooses one element per interrogation and records its identity. For static detuning, the echo rephases the spin, whereas the pooled twirl exhibits a contrast zero and a phase-reversed revival. Conditioning on the randomization record recovers opposite Ramsey fringes at the pooled zero. At 200 µs and 1.25 kHz, the finite-pulse model predicts a conditional Y probability difference of 0.941 ± 0.008 for 1,000 shots per branch. Each branch retains roughly 94% transverse contrast, and the pooled contrast is 0.0025. These values are prospective. Earlier experiments in nuclear magnetic resonance, quantum sensing, photonic interferometry and superconducting processors establish the underlying operations. We propose a common measurement that distinguishes their mechanisms and tests how far that distinction extends to spectroscopy-induced decoherence.

### Historical context

Hahn observed a macroscopic induction signal return after dephasing across spins with different precession frequencies. Haeberlen and Waugh later developed the average-Hamiltonian treatment of cyclic control. The latter formalism already explains selective retention of interactions, including the limitations due to finite cycling times. In quantum control, Zanardi formulated symmetrization of evolutions and Viola and Knill analyzed random paths on a control group. A random path within an individual sequence and a classical mixture of complete sequences can both appear in one experiment. [Hahn, 1950](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.80.580) · [Haeberlen and Waugh, 1968](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.175.453) · [Zanardi, 1999](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0375-9601%2899%2900365-5) · [Viola and Knill, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.060502).

The terminology entered through entanglement purification. Werner studied bipartite states invariant under identical local rotations in 1989. The Bennett–Brassard–Popescu–Schumacher–Smolin–Wootters preprint of November 1995 prescribed a random bilateral rotation, applying the same random SU(2) to the partners of each pair. The first arXiv version of Bennett, DiVincenzo, Smolin and Wootters, dated 23 April 1996, explicitly calls that operation a “twirl.” It preserves singlet weight while symmetrizing the triplet sector. This dates a documented use of the noun. Oral priority and first use of the exact phrase “quantum twirl” remain open. [Werner, 1989](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.40.4277) · [November 1995 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9511027), published as [PRL 76, 722 (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.76.722) · [April 1996 first version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9604024v1#page=22), published as [PRA 54, 3824 (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.54.3824).

### Proposed measurement

Write H = ℏδZ/2 and φ = δT. Prepare +X and compare two experiments with the optical probe off. The echo composes the +H and −H propagators for T/2 each and restores the analysis frame. Their product is identity. The channel twirl chooses I or X fairly, brackets the free interval with that operation and its inverse, and pools outcomes. Acting on transverse coherence, the pooled map multiplies by cos φ. The two branches individually give exp(±iφ). The ideal contrast is |cos φ|; measuring signed quadratures retains the phase reversal. The average Hamiltonian of the two signs is zero in both constructions as an algebraic statement. Exponentiation and taking the channel average give different physical predictions. With noncommuting terms, the leading time average also ceases to specify the exact echo propagator.

Use the approximately 1.789 GHz clock transition. Choose T = 200 µs of free evolution, adopting the time scale of the 0.2 ms spectroscopy probe in Fig. 2 of [Clos et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.112.113003). The numerical model assumes a microwave Rabi rate of 50 kHz, a +1% amplitude error, 100 Hz rms shot-static Gaussian detuning jitter and 95% common visibility. These are calibration scenarios. The detuning stays on during the 10 µs π pulses, and each frame is closed with the same π pulse that opened it, so the two pulse-area errors add. The comparison setting reverses the closing pulse’s drive phase (`closing=-1` in `echo_twirl.py`). This inverts a resonant pulse with the same amplitude error. With detuning present, only the drive term changes sign. The Freiburg registry's 114 µs Ramsey value refers to the stretch state without line triggering, and its quoted high carrier rates refer to Raman drive. Measure clock-state pulse area, readout asymmetry and probe-off coherence on the day. [Freiburg parameter registry](https://single-25mg-plus-d679f9.pages.uni-freiburg.de/content/experiment/parameters.yaml).

The primary statistic is ΔP = (Yᵢ − Yₓ)/2 with standard error √[pᵢ(1−pᵢ)/nᵢ + pₓ(1−pₓ)/nₓ], where pⱼ = (1+Yⱼ)/2. This gives ΔP = 0.94094 and SE = 0.00757 for nᵢ = nₓ = 1,000. Use the observed counts for a random split. As a refocusing control, at 2,000 independent binary shots per arm in X, the predicted difference at the null is 0.474 in bright-state probability, with projection-noise standard error 0.012. The small predicted residual contrast itself is below this counting resolution. Estimate the signed quadratures and their uncertainty before inferring a magnitude near zero. Interleave arms and analysis phases; retain the frame bit and experimental time. Compare branch weights, conditional Y, pooled X and the revival. Characterize the unequal pulse duty cycles and verify that the result tracks δT when δ and T are varied independently. A time-matched reference can apply the imposed detuning only during the programmed free intervals and pad the shorter sequence on resonance.

### What could constitute a research result?

The three ideal fringes offer a teaching experiment with a quantitative control. For a research article, introduce a calibrated second field transverse to Z so the two toggling Hamiltonians fail to commute. For H± = ℏ(εX ± δZ)/2, half-interval ordering produces a leading Y rotation with opposite sign when the order is reversed, proportional to εδT² (angle εδT²/4 for ε and δ in angular frequency; `test_echo_twirl.py` reproduces this with the model propagator). A fair channel mixture is independent of the enumeration of its two branches. Reverse the echo order with a common final frame, measure a quadrature sensitive to the Y rotation (e.g. Z for +X input), vary T, and reverse each applied field. Record pulse-only controls to bound order-dependent detuning and pulse transients. Compare observations with exact finite-pulse propagation using independently calibrated parameters. This is a proposal for an identifiable residual and scaling test, not a priority claim about commutator spectroscopy.

A complementary optical arm can tune the scattering probability and measure leakage separately. The statistical identifiability limit is consequential: two mechanisms producing the same ion channel give identical ion-only probabilities for all probe states and measurements. Extra records, detected photons, a leakage-sensitive measurement, or time correlations can supply discriminatory information when the hypotheses differ on those observables. Specify what is collected and the conditioning efficiency. The 2014 result supplies the scattering-sensitive sequence; the published optical spectra do not constitute measurements of the proposed randomized comparison.

### Scope of the literature claim

This is a targeted primary-source review. It establishes experimental precedents for the tools, their combination and several observables. It cannot establish exhaustive priority for the exact matched single-ion sequence. A methods or teaching paper could center on the conditional/pooled comparison. A research manuscript would require new data and a quantified physical inference beyond the ideal null.
